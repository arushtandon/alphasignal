## Notes

- Instruments enter when they have the full lookback plus 63 sessions of history: `RTY=F` from 2018, `BTC-USD` from 2015/2016, `ETH-USD` from 2018/2019 depending on the lookback.
- Yahoo `=F` series are front-month continuous contracts; roll jumps are in the signal and the mark. Roll cost is charged explicitly per contract-year on held notional. Rates are traded on the ZN/ZB/ZF price series; IBKR micro Treasury contracts are yield-quoted (sign inversion is an execution item).
- Spot `BTC-USD`/`ETH-USD` include weekend bars; the SPY calendar drives marks, so weekend crypto moves land on Monday.
- Per-instrument P&L is in units of portfolio capital after the portfolio scalar; costs include half a round trip per side traded plus roll.
