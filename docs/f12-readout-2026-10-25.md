## Notes

- Index series for Nikkei, Hang Seng, DAX, CAC and FTSE are cash indices; the S&P 500 uses the `ES=F` front-month series. Micro-futures costs are applied to all; `ISF.L` uses a 0.10% ETF round trip.
- Equity universes: a name without a price file is dropped (see coverage); delisted names exit at their last available close.
- UK Piotroski eligibility covers only quarterly filers (half-year filers are `INELIGIBLE_STATEMENT_CADENCE`), so the UK value + quality book is a small survivor-leaning sample and carries a coverage caveat regardless of verdict.
- Fundamentals reported in a currency other than the market currency are converted at the latest cached FX rate (approximation; affects ranks only).
