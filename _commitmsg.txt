Make backtest non-blocking to stop /api/analyze 502s (v7.7.1b)

The synchronous walk-forward backtest was starving the event loop and
spiking RSS on the 512MB Render Starter tier, causing Render to return
502 (dynamic-paid-error) for /api/analyze at ~2s.

- backtestSignal + simulateTradeExitTrailing are now async and yield to the
  event loop every 16 iterations (setImmediate). This keeps health checks
  passing and lets the GC reclaim the short-lived tech objects.
- Default entryStep 2 (halves the entry scan work).
- /api/analyze and refresh-pnl updated to await the now-async helpers.
